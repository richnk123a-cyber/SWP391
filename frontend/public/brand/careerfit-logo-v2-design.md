# CareerFit — logo v2

Logo được thiết kế theo hệ thống CareerFit: tuyển dụng IT, gợi ý việc làm theo kỹ năng và lộ trình phát triển cho sinh viên/Fresher.

- Biểu tượng: chữ C kết hợp dấu tích hướng lên, thể hiện sự phù hợp và tiến bộ nghề nghiệp.
- Bảng màu định hướng: xanh dương `#285DE4`, navy `#172132`.
- Tệp: `careerfit-logo-v2.png`, nền trong suốt, bố cục ngang.
- Phù hợp nhất với nền trắng hoặc sáng để chữ navy dễ đọc.
- Tạo bằng công cụ image_gen tích hợp. Đây là ảnh PNG; không phải tệp vector.

## Prompt đã dùng

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final horizontal logo for CareerFit, an IT recruitment and skill-development platform for Vietnamese students and fresh graduates.
Primary request: design one original polished logo that communicates career fit, skill matching and upward career progress, suitable for the existing restrained blue and navy web interface.
Subject: a distinctive simple geometric C-shaped symbol with a rising checkmark integrated into its open right side through considered negative space. The checkmark suggests successful skill/job matching and an upward career path. The mark must feel cohesive, not a collection of separate clipart.
Composition: one horizontal logo lockup only. Compact symbol at left, exact wordmark "CareerFit" at right. Balanced optical spacing, emblem approximately the height of the wordmark or slightly taller. Logo occupies most of a wide canvas with modest clear margins.
Style: crisp flat vector-like brand design, very clean geometric construction, confident professional technology brand with friendly subtly rounded corners; strong recognizability at navbar and favicon sizes.
Color palette: emblem royal blue #285DE4 with navy #172132 only if essential. Word "Career" navy #172132 and "Fit" royal blue #285DE4. Use solid colors only.
Text verbatim: "CareerFit", capital C and capital F, all other letters lowercase, clean modern geometric sans-serif, semibold, highly legible, one baseline.
Background: true transparent alpha. All spaces around and inside symbol must be transparent.
Constraints: only the one emblem and one wordmark, no tagline, no explanatory labels, no presentation board, no mockup, no extra logo versions, no watermark, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D, no stock briefcase, no graduation cap, no fine details. Deliver a usable transparent logo asset, not a brand poster.
