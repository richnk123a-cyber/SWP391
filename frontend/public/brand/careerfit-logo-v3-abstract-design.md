## Prompt đã dùng

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: refined abstract horizontal brand logo for CareerFit, a platform matching IT students and fresh graduates with jobs and skill-development roadmaps.
Input image: the supplied previous logo is an edit target for a substantial redesign. Retain exact brand name "CareerFit", a blue/navy palette, and transparent background; redesign the emblem and typography.
Primary request: make the identity noticeably more abstract, distinctive and professional, with the restraint and precision of a high-end technology consultancy identity.
Emblem: two complementary solid geometric ribbon-like shapes fitting together around an intentional clean negative-space channel. Form one compact, balanced abstract symbol with a quiet sense of forward motion and alignment, suggesting talent meeting opportunity. Use elegant straight segments with a few controlled curved joins, consistent geometry and substantial shapes. No literal letters, no C-outline, no checkmark, no arrowhead, no puzzle piece, no infinity sign, no stock chain-link icon. The design should have an ownable silhouette and work at small sizes.
Typography: exact wordmark "CareerFit" in a sophisticated modern sans-serif with medium weight, restrained proportions, optically considered spacing. Entire wordmark navy #172132, one baseline. Emblem solid royal blue #285DE4. Avoid oversized heavy rounded lettering.
Composition: one horizontal lockup, symbol on left and wordmark on right. Emblem only about 1.15 times capital-letter height; comfortable spacing between symbol and text, generous clear space. Wide canvas around 3:1.
Style: minimal flat vector-like identity, sharp clean edges, exact optical balance, 2D solid fills only.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha.
Constraints: output only one final usable logo, no presentation board, no extra versions, no tagline, no labels, no decorative dots, no gradients, no bevels, no shadows, no 3D, no watermark.
